Our Story

More Than a Café.
A Place to Feel at Home.

Urban Solace brings together the things that make an evening worth remembering — honest comfort food, unhurried conversation, a little culture, and a peaceful setting just across from Ulsoor Lake.

Whether you drop in for waffles and iced tea, settle in over burgers and pasta with friends, or stay late for a comedy set or a poetry reading, the café is built for lingering. Cozy, homely, and full of character — it is the kind of place regulars call their own.
